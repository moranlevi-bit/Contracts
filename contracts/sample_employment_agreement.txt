EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into on January 15, 2026 between
Northwind Analytics Ltd., a data analytics software company ("Company"),
and Dana Cohen ("Employee").

1. Position. Employee will serve as Senior Data Engineer.

2. Compensation. Company shall pay Employee a base salary of USD 145,000
per year, payable in equal monthly installments.

3. Bonus. Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to
15% of base salary, based on individual and company goals, plus a one-time
signing bonus of USD 10,000 payable with the first paycheck.

4. Term. This Agreement continues until terminated by either party with
30 days' written notice.
